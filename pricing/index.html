A FEW QUESTIONS

How credits
actually work.

What's a credit?+

Every plan includes a monthly credit budget. Each model draws a different number of credits per generation, based on what it actually costs to run — lighter, faster models cost fewer credits; longer or higher-fidelity models cost more.

Why does the number of generations vary so much?+

It depends on which models you use. A plan's "up to" figure assumes you only use its lightest model; the lower figure assumes you only use its heaviest. Most people land somewhere in between.

Can I switch plans?+

Yes. Move up when you need more models or more room to create, move down when you don't — changes apply from your next billing cycle.

Do unused credits roll over?+

Not currently. Credits refresh at the start of each billing cycle.

Which plan should I start with?+

Starter if you're exploring. Creator or Pro once you know which models fit your work. Studio once you need everything, including Veo 3.1.
