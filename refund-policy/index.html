LEGALREFUND POLICY

Refund
Policy.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy covers subscription plans and model add-ons purchased through Hollowick (hollowick.app). It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

1. No refunds

Subscription fees and model add-on fees are non-refundable, including for partial billing periods, unused generation credits, and unused model add-ons. When you cancel a subscription or remove an add-on, you keep access to it for the remainder of the period you already paid for, but we do not issue a refund or credit for the time or credits you don't use.

This reflects that credits are usage-based and plans are billed in advance; it does not limit any refund or withdrawal right that applicable law makes mandatory and non-waivable.

2. Free trial

Your first 7 days on Hollowick are a free trial and are never charged. No payment is collected to start a trial, and the trial does not automatically convert into a paid plan — if you don't choose a paid plan by the time your trial ends, your account simply loses generation access until you do. Because nothing is charged during the trial, there is nothing to refund from it.

3. Cancelling your plan

You can cancel your subscription or remove a model add-on at any time from your account's billing settings, which opens Stripe's billing portal. Cancelling stops future billing; it does not retroactively refund the current period, per Section 1 above.

4. Billing errors

If you believe you were charged incorrectly — for example, a duplicate charge, or a charge after you had already cancelled — contact us and we will investigate and correct a genuine billing error. This is a correction of a mistake, not a discretionary refund, and is handled on a case-by-case basis once we've confirmed what happened.

5. Contact

For billing questions, reach us at hello@hollowick.app.
