LEGALPRIVACY POLICY

Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Hollowick collects when you use hollowick.app and api.hollowick.app (together, the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. "Hollowick," "we," or "us" refers to the operator of the Service, based in Switzerland. This policy is written to comply with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for visitors in the European Economic Area or UK, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable.

1. What data we collect

Account data: your email address and a securely hashed version of your password (we never store your password in plain text).

Billing data: your subscription plan, billing status, and the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers associated with your account. Full payment card details are collected and processed directly by Stripe, our payment processor — Hollowick never receives or stores your full card number.

Usage and content data: the prompts, reference images, and other inputs you submit for video generation; the resulting generated videos and their metadata (model used, duration, status); and records of your credit usage and generation history, so that we can run the Service and enforce plan limits.

Communications data: your email verification status, and a log of the transactional and account-lifecycle emails we send you (such as sign-up confirmation, trial reminders, and billing notices), sent through our email provider, Resend.

Technical data: standard server log information such as IP address, browser/device information, and timestamps, collected automatically when you use the Service, primarily for security, debugging, and abuse prevention.

Session data: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, scoped to the hollowick.app domain. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How we use your data

We use the data above to: create and maintain your account; authenticate you and keep your session secure; process subscription payments and add-on purchases through Stripe; send your prompts and reference material to the relevant third-party video generation model to produce your Output; enforce plan credit limits and model access; send transactional and lifecycle emails (verification, trial start/expiry reminders, billing notices); detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service; and maintain, secure, and improve the Service.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your prompts or generated videos to train our own models without your consent.

3. Who we share data with

We share data with the following categories of service providers, each acting as a data processor under instructions from Hollowick: Stripe (payment processing and billing), Resend (transactional email delivery), our database and hosting providers (storing account, usage, and generation records), and third-party AI video generation providers (receiving the prompts, reference images, and parameters needed to generate the video you request).

These providers may process data outside Switzerland, including in the United States or European Union. Where that happens, we rely on contractual safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) or the provider's own recognized compliance framework to protect your data in transit and at rest. We do not share your data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We may also disclose data where required by law, to enforce our Terms of Service, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Hollowick, our users, or the public.

4. Data retention

We retain account and billing data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations. Generation history and usage records are retained for as long as needed to provide the Service (including displaying your past generations to you) and may be deleted on request as described below. If you close your account, we will delete or anonymize personal data that we are not legally required to retain within a reasonable period.

5. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; correct inaccurate data; request deletion of your data; request a copy of your data in a portable format; and object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@hollowick.app. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. If you are in the EEA or UK and believe we have not addressed your request properly, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed password storage, and access controls on our infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at and may not be used by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we learn that we have collected data from someone under 18, we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and for material changes we will make reasonable efforts to notify active users by email.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests regarding your personal data, can be sent to privacy@hollowick.app.
